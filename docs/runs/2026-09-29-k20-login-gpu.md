## Why k matters in PSBD

PSBD scores a sample by its prediction shift under stochastic forward passes, PSU = 1 - (1/k) * sum_i P_c(x; p, theta_i') / P_c(x; theta). This is a Monte Carlo estimate of an expectation over the mask distribution, and the paper fixes k = 3. A larger k lowers the variance of the score, which matters most at low poison rates where the clean and triggered score distributions overlap. `scripts/paper/fig_forward_passes.py` (H24) plots AUROC and TPR against k. Before this run it could only draw k up to 3 for most models, because only 17 of the 59 clearing ViT models carried a `before_attention_norm_token_mask_k20` cache.

## Deviation 1, 1 rate instead of the 10 rate ladder

Each model is swept at k = 20 at the single rate the adaptive rule chose at k = 3 for that placement, not at the whole ladder. The adaptive rule (`select_rate_adaptively`, target 0.8) picks the smallest rate whose mean clean-validation shift ratio over passes reaches the target. That mean is an expectation over masks, so it does not depend on k. Only the variance of the score does. The rate chosen at k = 3 is therefore the rate the rule would choose at k = 20 up to sampling noise, and the figure asks what more sampling buys at a fixed disturbance. The pilot caches hold 10 rates and are left alone. `fig_forward_passes.py` needed no change for this, because `collect_cell` already asks `cache_holds_rate` for the cell's adaptive rate only.

## Deviation 2, k = 1 to 20 by slicing

`fig_forward_passes.py` now draws every integer k from 1 to 20 for the long curve, read as the first k passes of the 20 cached passes (`per_pass_probs[:k]`, shape (k, n), into `psu_ratio_from_cache`). The masks are iid, so the first k passes are the same estimator as a k-pass run. They are not the same draws. `defenses.inference.compute_dropout_pass_probs` seeds once per split and then draws k passes for each batch in turn, so a k = 3 run and a k = 20 run share masks for the first batch only. Measured on `vit_cifar10_badnet_a2o_0_01` at rate 0.8, the first 64 samples differ by at most 0.0016 in probability (bfloat16 noise) between the 2 caches and the next 64 by up to 0.109 (different draws). The k values that come from slicing are 1 to 19. k = 20 is the cache itself. The macros keep k in 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20 so their names stay stable. The rate is fixed at the one chosen at k = 3.

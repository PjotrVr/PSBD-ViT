\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Input-level detectors.}
The defenses closest to ours decide per input at test time from the deployed model and a small clean set. STRIP \cite{gao2019strip} superimposes clean images and reads prediction entropy, SCALE-UP \cite{guo2023scale} amplifies pixels and IBD-PSC \cite{hou2024ibdpsc} normalization parameters and read what survives, TeCo \cite{liu2023teco} reads how much the corruption severity at which a prediction flips varies across corruption types, CD-L \cite{huang2023distilling} distills a minimal input mask, Beatrix \cite{ma2023beatrix} reads Gram-matrix deviations, TED \cite{mo2024ted} reads the rank trajectory of an input's neighbors across layers and SentiNet \cite{chou2020sentinet} transplants a salient region. PSBD \cite{li2025psbd} belongs to the consistency family with STRIP, SCALE-UP, TeCo and IBD-PSC, which perturb the model or its input and read how stable the prediction is. PSBD was introduced and evaluated as a detector of poisoned training samples, and we apply its score per input at test time. To our knowledge no earlier work treats the perturbation site of such a detector as a design choice, as it must be on a ViT. Doan et al.~\cite{doan2023vitpatch} detect backdoor inputs on ViTs by randomly dropping or shuffling patches before the positional encoding and reading whether the prediction holds. Their patch dropping is token masking at the input only, while PSBD-TM masks tokens at the attention input of every block.

\paragraph{Training-time defenses.}
Detectors that need the poisoned training pool \cite{tran2018spectral,chen2018activation,tang2021demon,hayase2021spectre} and defenses that retrain or prune the model \cite{liu2018finepruning,li2021nad,wu2021anp,zeng2022ibau,zheng2022clp} answer a different question and need access we do not assume.

\paragraph{Mechanistic work on ViT backdoors.}
Karayal\c{c}in et al.~\cite{karayalcin2026backdoor} find a trigger direction in ViT activations from paired clean and triggered images and remove the backdoor by orthogonalizing it out of the weights. They present this as a diagnostic that assumes knowledge of the trigger rather than as a defense, and our direction ablation of \cref{sec:mechanism:direction} builds on theirs. Activation patching \cite{zhang2024patching} is the tool we use to locate the backdoor by depth.

\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Models and training.}
Every model is a torchvision \cite{paszke2019pytorch} ViT-B/16 or Swin-S with ImageNet weights, fine-tuned for \TrainingEpochs{} epochs with Adam \cite{kingma2015adam} at a constant learning rate of $10^{-4}$ and weight decay $10^{-4}$, batch size \TrainingBatchSize{}, no dropout and no augmentation beyond the dataset's normalization. Every input is resized to \ModelInputSize{} by \ModelInputSize{} pixels before the backbone.

\paragraph{Datasets and attacks.}
The datasets are CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100 \cite{krizhevsky2009cifar}, GTSRB \cite{stallkamp2012gtsrb} and Tiny ImageNet \cite{le2015tiny}. The attacks are BadNets \cite{gu2017badnets}, Blend \cite{chen2017targeted}, WaNet \cite{nguyen2021wanet}, BPP \cite{wang2022bpp}, the low-frequency attack LF \cite{zeng2021lf}, TaCT \cite{tang2021demon}, SIG \cite{barni2019sig}, Label-Consistent \cite{turner2019label} and Adaptive-Blend \cite{qi2023adaptive}, each at poison rates of 1\%, 5\% and 10\% of the training set. \Cref{app:attacks} describes every attack and the exact trigger and parameters we used.

\paragraph{Which models are evaluated.}
A backdoored model enters the detection results only if its ASR is at least \AsrBar{} and its clean accuracy sits no further than \CleanDropBar{} below the benign reference on the same dataset. A detector cannot be credited or blamed for a backdoor that was never planted. Of the \PanelCellsTotal{} models we trained, \PanelCellsClearing{} pass this bar. Every result is read on the \PanelCellsCached{} of them that carry every placement we compare, and \cref{app:results} lists each with its ASR, clean accuracy and detection numbers.

\paragraph{Splits and uncertainty.}
A fixed permutation of each test set gives a clean validation set of \HeldoutSize{} images for the threshold and the rate rule, and an analysis pool for the paired clean and triggered sets. Nothing from the validation set enters the analysis pool. Every gain between two placements is a paired difference on the same model, averaged over models, with a 95\% bootstrap interval \cite{efron1993bootstrap} over models from \BootstrapResamples{} resamples.
